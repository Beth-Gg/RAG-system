
# PRM (Participant Resource Management)

## Overview
The PRM tool is designed to capture and manage participant data efficiently. It stores personal and business information for participants and enterprises, tracks each participant's training status, and provides dynamic reporting capabilities.

---
## Types of Users in PRM

### Admin Users
Admin users have special rights and privileges, allowing them to create and delete regular users within the organization.

### Regular Users
Regular users have standard access to the tool’s features.

---
## How to Access the PRM Tool (Login and Signup)

### To Login:
1. Navigate to [https://icipe.vercel.app/](https://icipe.vercel.app/) (best used with Chrome browser).
2. Enter your username and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

When you open the provided link, a login box appears where you need to enter your email and password, then click the **Sign in** button.

---
## Dashboard View
Upon logging in, you will see the main dashboard, which includes different sections of the tool. These are the main functionalities available:

### Adding Participants
1. On the **left panel**, click on the **Participants** option.
2. Click the **Add Participant** button on the **top right corner**.
3. A screen appears asking you to fill out the participant’s information.
4. Once you finish filling out the information, click the **Submit** button.
5. Continue entering participant data by clicking **Employment**.
6. Enter the **Employment Information** of the participant and select **Save**.
7. Continue entering participant data by clicking **Finance**.
8. Enter the **Financial Information** of the participant and click **Save**.
9. Add geographical data of the participant by clicking **Geography**.
10. Fill in the **Geographical Data** and click **Save**. (Note: Each field appears after selecting the first dropdown.)
11. Enter participant's technology usage data by selecting **Technology**.
12. Enter **Technology Familiarity** and click **Save**.

---
## Adding Participant's Business Details
1. On the **top right side**, select **Business** on the participant detail page.
2. Fill out all necessary fields and **Save**.
3. Add or remove businesses from your account using the **Add Page**.

---
## Creating Groups
1. Select **Group** from the left panel.
2. Click **Add Group** on the **top right corner**.
3. Fill out the **Group Information** and **Save**.

**Note:** The **Group Representative** dropdown pulls names from the list of already registered participants. If the representative's profile includes business details, these will automatically populate for the group.

### Adding/Removing Members from Groups
- To add members to an already created group:
  1. Go to the **Group** section and click **Add Members**.
  2. You will see a list of registered participants. Check the box for those you want to add.
  3. Click **Add to Group**.
  4. View added members by clicking **View Members**.
  5. Remove members by selecting the participant and clicking **Remove from Group**.

---
## Creating Users
1. Select **Users** from the **left sidebar**.
2. Click **Add Users** on the **top right corner**.
3. Populate user information and **Save**.
